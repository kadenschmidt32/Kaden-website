Congress-Bundestag Youth Exchange (CBYX)

The Congress-Bundestag Youth Exchange (CBYX) is a fully-funded scholarship sponsored by the U.S. Congress and the German Bundestag. It sends American students to Germany for a year of cultural immersion, intensive language learning, academic study, and professional internship experience — all while living with a German host family.
